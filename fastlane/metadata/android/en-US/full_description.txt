A decluttered, context-aware home screen that respects your time and privacy.

Your apps stay where you put them. Only three dock shortcuts follow your context, detected on your device. No tracking, no ads, open source.

<b>A home screen that does not move</b>
Clock, weather, your next calendar event and up to six favorite apps, in the order you chose, followed by a search line. Nothing reorders them: no ranking, no learning from your habits.

<b>Every app, quickly</b>
Type to search, or slide along the alphabet to reach a letter with one hand.

<b>Profiles, if you want them</b>
Pick none, or exactly three (for example Work, Home and Out). Each profile has a dock of three apps you choose. Only the dock changes with the profile; your favorites never do.

<b>Switching you can predict</b>
Change profile by hand at any time, or let the launcher switch when you connect to, or leave, a Wi-Fi network you saved. It never switches while you are using the screen, and automatic switching can be turned off.

<b>Works with nothing granted</b>
Without network, location or calendar access, you can still open all your apps.

<b>Privacy</b>
No account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting, no telemetry. Wi-Fi network names are read on the device and never sent. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, off until you choose a city: the coordinates of that city, rounded to about 1 km, are sent; your device's own position never is.

Precise location is only asked if you turn on automatic switching, because Android reveals the connected Wi-Fi network name only to apps with precise location. Approximate location is only asked if you tap "Use my location" for the weather; the position itself is neither stored nor sent.
